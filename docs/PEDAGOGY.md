# Pedagogical Model

## Learning sequence

**context → sound → meaning → pattern → guided use → independent communication**

## Content requirements

Every published learning element must be specific, natural, level-appropriate and connected to a practical goal. Examples, distractors and answers are reviewed for ambiguity. Synonyms are validated per sense and register.

## Progression

Content develops from recognition to comprehension, guided production, free production and automatic use. Communicative functions such as asking, reacting, expressing opinions and emotions progress from basic to natural and professional Dutch.

## Practice

The exercise bank mixes vocabulary, listening, grammar, word order, writing, natural responses, visual context and real-life situations. Feedback identifies the relevant language pattern rather than merely marking an answer wrong.
