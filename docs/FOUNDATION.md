# web_nl_learn Foundation

**Version:** 19.2  
**Status:** Living document

## Purpose

This document defines the linguistic, pedagogical, product and engineering principles of **web_nl_learn**. The platform evolves through addition, refinement and validation, never by sacrificing proven quality or previously validated content.

## Vision

web_nl_learn teaches Dutch as an interconnected language system. It combines structured courses, pronunciation, grammar, vocabulary, verbs, exercises, natural conversation and a knowledge graph in one calm and accessible experience.

## Core principles

1. **Single source of truth.** Definitions, conjugations, examples, pronunciation notes and collocations are stored once and reused.
2. **Natural Dutch first.** Frequent and idiomatic language is taught before literal or unnecessarily formal alternatives.
3. **Systems, not lists.** Learners discover patterns, lexical families, collocations and grammatical relationships.
4. **Meaning by sense.** Definitions and synonyms belong to a specific meaning and are never copied automatically across senses.
5. **Communication drives grammar.** Every rule supports a real communicative task.
6. **Pronunciation is part of vocabulary.** Words are learned with sound, stress, spelling patterns and listening practice.
7. **Errors teach.** Feedback explains the pattern and gives a natural alternative.
8. **Levels are views.** A0–C2 organize learning, but knowledge is reusable across the platform.
9. **Quality over volume.** Unverified, vague or incomplete content is not published.
10. **No regression.** New releases preserve validated content, exercises, images, routes and user trust.

## Product principle

> The sophistication belongs in the architecture; simplicity belongs in the learner experience.
